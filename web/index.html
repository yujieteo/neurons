Neurons

The 46,080 MLP neurons of SmolLM2-135M, swept over 10 million tokens. Claude explored 600 of them. The model runs on this page, in Goose compiled to WebAssembly.

        How it works

        A neuron is one of the 1,536 values per layer between the up projection and the down projection of the MLP: silu(gate_proj(x)) × up_proj(x). The sweep ran the model over text from FineWeb-Edu and Cosmopedia, and kept each neuron's mean, standard deviation and top contexts. Claude labelled the neurons that stand out most in each layer, from their contexts and from text the model writes when they are steered.

        The page fetches the weights from Hugging Face and runs the forward pass in engine/llama.goose, compiled through C to WebAssembly.
